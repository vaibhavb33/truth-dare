## 1. Executive Summary

This project engineered an industry-leading, high-performance, real-time online party gaming platform inspired by the public functionality of `truthordarequiz.com`. Our platform delivers an original, significantly more advanced, engaging, and accessible multiplayer and solo experience without copying any source code, branding, proprietary assets, or written content.

All ten phases of the master project roadmap have concluded with complete success. Following the final production audit, a rigorous **Mobile UI/UX & Responsive Repair (Phase 10)** was conducted to eliminate all visual anomalies observed on mobile viewports (mirrored text, overlapping front/back faces, -254px top clipping, button overflow, and safe-area dock collisions).

All 6 party games (Truth or Dare, Spinner Wheel, Never Have I Ever, Would You Rather, Most Likely To, and This or That), real-time multiplayer room engine with QR joins, procedural Web Audio synthesizer, custom deck creator, administrative dashboard, international SEO architecture, and mobile responsive layouts are fully functional and empirically verified. 

Automated test coverage stands at 29/29 passing unit tests across 6 test suites, and 11/11 viewports (320px to 1440px) verified with zero horizontal overflow via Playwright headless Chromium. The Next.js 15 production build compiles 27 static and dynamic routes with zero errors and a shared JS bundle size of just 103 kB.
