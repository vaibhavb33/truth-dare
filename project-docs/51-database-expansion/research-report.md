# Phase 2: Party Game Content Research Report
**Project:** Play Truth & Dare (https://playtruthndare.com)  
**Date:** November 2026  
**Methodology:** Approved Web Search Tools, Thematic Clustering & Quality Assurance  

---

## 1. Research Objectives & Guidelines
In accordance with user guidelines:
- All research utilized approved live web search tools without web scraping or unauthorized data harvesting.
- Questions are curated into structured categories, difficulty ratings, content ratings (`G`, `PG-13`, `R-18`), and game formats.
- Every question was screened for uniqueness, engagement value, humor, and natural phrasing.
- Strict duplicate filtering applied: no redundant questions or low-value filler.

---

## 2. Research Themes & Thematic Breakdown

### Theme A: Most Likely To (Target: 500+ Unique Questions)
1. **Funny & Relatable Clumsiness**:
   - Trip over flat surfaces, lose keys inside hand, reply 3 weeks later with "sorry just saw this", argue with self-checkout.
2. **Social Media & Tech Habits**:
   - Accidental 3-year-old Instagram like, 3 AM rabbit holes, rehearse phone calls out loud, send text *about* someone *to* that person.
3. **Party Animals & Night Out**:
   - Karaoke with strangers, friend with the Uber driver, "one more drink" at 3 AM, lose shoes, sneak meals into cinemas.
4. **Career & Adulting**:
   - Fall asleep in jeans, accidentally set kitchen on fire, survive zombie apocalypse by pure luck, lie on resume and still get promoted.
5. **Dating & Romance**:
   - Stalk ex's new partner on a burner account, ghost someone over their food order, text an ex during a weak moment.

### Theme B: Never Have I Ever (Target: 500+ Unique Questions)
1. **Daily Quirks & Lazy Habits**:
   - Fridge door grazing, wearing pajamas 3 days straight, talking to pets in full human speech, pretending to listen to a movie while snoring.
2. **Public Blunders**:
   - Waving back at a stranger who was waving at someone else, calling boss/teacher "Mom", pulling a door that clearly says "PUSH".
3. **Tech & Screen Fails**:
   - Dropping phone on face in bed, sending text to wrong group, binging show after swearing to wait for a friend.
4. **Food & Travel Adventures**:
   - Eating off the floor (5-second rule), putting metal in the microwave, missing a flight/train by seconds.

### Theme C: Would You Rather (Target: 500+ Unique Dilemmas)
1. **Absurd & Unhinged**:
   - Bear-crawl everywhere vs steam from ears like a cartoon train; 3 stacked hats vs no socks forever.
2. **Thought-Provoking & Philosophical**:
   - Know date of death vs cause of death; rewind conversations vs fast forward; master all instruments vs speak all languages.
3. **Social Dilemmas**:
   - Browser history read to family vs boss; 20 minutes early vs 20 minutes late; clear glass house vs pitch black cave.

### Theme D: This or That (Target: 500+ Unique Pairs)
1. **Lifestyle & Habits**:
   - Coffee vs Tea, Early Bird vs Night Owl, Texting vs Calling, Paper Books vs Kindle.
2. **Travel & Leisure**:
   - Beach Vacation vs Mountain Cabin, Strict Itinerary vs Winging It, Solo Travel vs Group Trip.
3. **Party & Debates**:
   - Pineapple on Pizza vs Never, Karaoke Solo vs Duet, Flight vs Invisibility, House Party vs Clubbing.
4. **Couples & Dating**:
   - Night Out vs Cozy Night In, Talk It Out Immediately vs Cool Down First, Big Wedding vs Eloping.

### Theme E: Truth or Dare (Target: 1,000 Truths + 1,000 Dares)
1. **Funny & Embarrassing**:
   - Most awkward internet search, worst first date memory, weirdest food combo, childish habits.
2. **Deep & Emotional**:
   - Biggest fear rarely spoken aloud, turning point that changed worldview, regret and lessons.
3. **Couples & Spicy**:
   - Secret turns-ons, memorable date night moments, flirty dares, relationship hot takes.
4. **Physical & Safe Dares**:
   - Impressions, funny accents, phone text roulette, dramatic pantomimes, quick choreography.
