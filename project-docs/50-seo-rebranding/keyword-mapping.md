## 2. Cannibalization Prevention Matrix

| Risk Scenario | Prevention Strategy |
|---|---|
| **Homepage (`/`) vs. TOD Arena (`/games/truth-or-dare`)** | The homepage targets high-level platform discovery ("Truth or Dare Online | Play With Friends Free") and links down to the 3D game arena ("Play Truth or Dare Online"). The homepage features room codes and curated category carousels; the arena handles gameplay state. |
| **Game Arena (`/games/truth-or-dare`) vs. Questions Bank (`/questions`)** | The game arena serves interactive card flipping with sound and countdown timers. The questions bank serves searchable, copyable lists of prompts with category filters and voice audio buttons. |
| **Localized Pages (`/es`, `/hi`, `/fr`, etc.) vs. English (`/`)** | Reciprocal hreflang tags declared on all alternate URLs ensure search engines serve the language-specific URL to native searchers without indexing conflicts. |

## 3. Title & Meta Description Quality Guidelines

1. **Character Boundaries:** Title tags are kept between 50–60 characters (max 580px width) to avoid truncation in Google SERPs. Meta descriptions are kept between 140–158 characters.
2. **Branding Suffix:** Consistent brand suffix `| Play Truth & Dare` or `— Play Truth & Dare` across all indexed pages.
3. **No Keyword Stuffing:** Natural sentence structures incorporating primary keywords alongside real player value propositions (free, zero ads, instant room codes, 100% free).
