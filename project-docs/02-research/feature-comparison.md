### A. Essential MVP (Minimum Viable Product — Core Launch)
These features represent the non-negotiable core required to provide an experience superior to `truthordarequiz.com` on day one:

1. **Instant Web Play:** Zero account registration, zero paywalls, instant game initialization in under 1 second.
2. **Core Game Loop:**
   * Binary choice interface ("Truth" vs. "Dare") with smooth 3D card flip animation.
   * Shuffle engine with anti-repetition memory (no duplicate cards during a single active session).
3. **Curated Starter Question Libraries (5 Distinct Decks):**
   * *Classic Party* (Clean, funny, all-ages safe).
   * *Teens & Hangouts* (Relatable social, school, drama prompts).
   * *Couples & Flirty* (Romantic, deep connection, playful intimacy).
   * *Spicy / After Dark 18+* (Explicit, bold, party daring with adult gate).
   * *Deep & Philosophical* (Introspective conversation starters).
4. **Local Pass & Play Roster:**
   * Host inputs player names.
   * Platform automatically manages turn rotation, displaying: *"It's Sarah's turn!"* with current turn count and skip tracker.
5. **Integrated Dare Timer:**
   * 15s, 30s, and 60s presets with visual progress bar and synthesized audio countdown ticks.
6. **Procedural Web Audio Engine:**
   * Zero-asset, zero-lag sound effects (card flip whoosh, suspense countdown, success chime, buzzer) generated via the native Web Audio API with an instant master mute toggle.
7. **Mobile Web Haptics:**
   * Native device vibration cues on card reveal, countdown expiration, and dare completion.
8. **Dark Mode & Responsive UI:**
   * Optimized touch targets (minimum 48×48px), high-contrast accessibility (WCAG AA), seamless viewport scaling.

---

### B. Important V1 (Multiplayer & Viral Growth)
Capabilities added immediately following MVP stabilization to capture social distribution and search traffic:

1. **Synchronized Multiplayer Rooms:**
   * Host creates a room and receives a unique 6-character room code and direct shareable link / QR code.
   * Guests join on their personal phones with zero install.
   * Host or active player flips the card; card state reflects across all connected screens in < 150ms.
2. **Live Social Reactions & Streamer Effects:**
   * In-room players tap floating emoji triggers (🔥, 😂, 😱, 👏) that burst across all connected screens.
3. **Consensus Dare Voting:**
   * When a dare finishes, room participants vote "Passed" or "Chickened Out" to award points or forfeits.
4. **Interactive Virtual Spinner Wheel:**
   * Customizable digital wheel / bottle spinner to randomly determine whose turn it is or pick a random dare consequence.
5. **Custom Deck Builder (Client-Side Storage):**
   * Users can draft, save, edit, and export their own custom Truths and Dares using browser `localStorage` and inject them directly into game sessions.
6. **Programmatic SEO Landing Pages:**
   * Dynamic landing pages for top search queries (`/truth-or-dare-for-couples`, `/dirty-truth-or-dare`, `/clean-truth-or-dare-for-kids`).
   * Complete Schema.org JSON-LD structured data (Game, Quiz, FAQPage, BreadcrumbList).

---

### C. Advanced V2 (Platform Expansion & Extended Game Suite)
Capabilities that transform the single-game utility into a complete party gaming ecosystem:

1. **Multi-Game Party Suite Expansion:**
   * *Never Have I Ever* (with finger-down / life counter mechanics).
   * *Would You Rather* (with live percentage statistics of global answers).
   * *Most Likely To* (with real-time room player nomination voting).
   * *This or That* (rapid-fire dilemma engine).
2. **AI Question Generator:**
   * Integrated LLM-powered prompt generator allowing players to input custom themes (e.g., "Tech Startup Office Party", "College Road Trip", "First Date") to generate 20 novel, contextual truths and dares instantly.
3. **Community Deck Marketplace:**
   * Public repository where users can browse, upvote, and play verified decks submitted by other players.
4. **Persistent User Accounts (Optional / Social Sign-In):**
   * Optional OAuth (Google / Apple) to save custom decks across devices and track lifetime party statistics without blocking anonymous instant play.

---

### D. Future Opportunities (Horizon 3 Exploration)
Long-term strategic differentiators:
1. **AI Virtual Game Master:** Synthesized conversational AI voice host that reads dares aloud, mediates disputes, and generates roast commentary.
2. **Direct WebRTC Video & Audio Mesh:** Optional picture-in-picture video feeds embedded directly in the multiplayer room for long-distance hangouts without needing third-party Discord/FaceTime apps.
3. **Twitch & Discord Streamer Extensions:** Live audience voting plugins where Twitch chat or Discord servers can submit and vote on dares for streamers in real time.
